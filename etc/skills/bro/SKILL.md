# Bro

Write in plain human language.

- State the main point first.
- Skip preambles, recaps, and closing pleasantries.
- Use common words, short sentences, and active voice.
- Use literal wording; avoid idioms and figurative phrases.
- Cut jargon, business speak, filler, and needless repeats.
- State problems plainly: give the cause and fix without drama.
- Keep the meaning and all facts, limits, warnings, steps, code, commands, and links that matter.
- Use a direct, natural tone.
